Every filehas a journey.

WZML-X is a Telegram bot that moves anything — a magnet, a link, a video — to wherever you need it, re-encoding and renaming on the way. Send one, and follow it all the way home.

or simply scroll ↓

Scene one

It begins with a message.

Every task is one of two directions. Leech brings the file back into Telegram. Mirror sends it to cloud storage — Google Drive or any rclone remote.

The engine

A prefix picks who fetches it.

No prefix means aria2 for plain links. qb hands magnets to qBittorrent, y sends video sites to yt-dlp. JDownloader and SABnzbd are there too — switched off until the owner enables them.

The details

Flags shape the result.

Add as many as you like, in any order — as long as the link comes first. Switch some on and watch the message grow.

    The control room

    Two settings menus decide how every journey behaves: yours (/usettings) and the owner’s (/bsettings). Each phone below replays the real menus — step by step, or tap through them yourself.

    Resolution order, for any setting that exists at several levels: task flag → your /usettings → bot config. A value you set yourself always beats the bot’s.

    When the story bends

    My config change did nothing.

    A value in /usettings overrides config.py permanently. Check there first and press Reset.

    It landed in the wrong chat.

    Only LEECH_LOG_CHAT (or -ud) is a primary destination. -up and your dump chats only receive copies. Use the full -100… id.

    My split size snapped back to 2 GB.

    Premium wasn’t detected when you saved it. Confirm PREMIUM_USER: true in the prompt, then enter it again.

    /cancel GID does nothing.

    Tap the /c_<gid> link on the task’s status message instead.

    jd, nzb or Seedr refuse to run.

    Those engines are switched off by default. Turn off DISABLE_JD, DISABLE_NZB or DISABLE_SEEDR in /bsettings.

    /mediainfo and /imdb are silent.

    They are plugins now. Enable them from /plugins.
